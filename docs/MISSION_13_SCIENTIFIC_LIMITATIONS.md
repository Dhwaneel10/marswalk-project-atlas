## 3. Detailed Limitations by Domain

### 3.1 Spatial Resolution & Sub-Pixel Terrain Blindspots
- **Macro-Topography (CTX 20m):** While USGS 20m CTX DTM provides excellent regional context for crater rims, delta scarps, and valleys, each pixel spans $20.0\,\text{m} \times 20.0\,\text{m}$ ($400\,\text{m}^2$).
- **Sub-Pixel Hazards:** Meter-scale boulders, fissures, deep sand ripples, and regolith shear failures are **completely invisible** at 20m resolution.
- **Micro-Topography (HiRISE 1m):** Available only over Hawksbill Gap. Even at 1m resolution, centimeter-scale loose pebbles and ankle rollover hazards remain unmapped.
- **Operational Requirement:** The astronaut visor HUD displays an advisory: *"Unresolved micro-hazards require in-situ visual confirmation."*

### 3.2 Rover Ground Truth vs. Human Walking Mechanics
- **Teramechanics Mismatch:** A 1,025 kg 6-wheel rocker-bogie rover (Perseverance) distributes weight across wide cleated wheels, minimizing sinkage on low-bearing soils.
- **Human Bipedal Gait:** An astronaut in a pressurized spacesuit exerts dynamic point loads through boot heels, creating distinct ground sinkage, shear failure, and ankle inversion hazards.
- **Disclosure:** The system displays: *"Rover ground truth demonstrates robotic traversability, but does NOT certify human foot travel."*

### 3.3 Physiological Biomechanics & Environmental Analogue Limits
- **Minetti Locomotion Analogue:** The non-linear slope penalty curve is adapted from Minetti et al. (2002) human gradient walking on Earth ($1.0\,g$), scaled for Martian gravity ($0.376\,g$) and pressurized suit joint torque resistance.
- **Apollo Lunar EVA Data:** Baseline metabolic expenditures ($320\,\text{kcal/h}$) and oxygen rates ($50\,\text{L/h}$) are derived from Apollo lunar surface telemetry (Waligora et al., 1975). The Martian environment differs due to atmospheric drag (slight, $\sim 750\,\text{Pa}$), convective cooling, and higher gravity ($0.38\,g$ vs $0.16\,g$).
- **Flight Rules:** Life-support capacity ($600\,\text{L}$ $O_2$, $120\,\text{L}$ emergency reserve) represents conceptual engineering sizing, not an active NASA flight program specification.

### 3.4 Radiation Telemetry: Honest Unknown Disclosure
- **Absence of Real-Time In-Situ Sensors:** The static orbital DTMs and archives integrated in this platform do not possess real-time galactic cosmic ray (GCR) or solar particle event (SPE) sensors for arbitrary ground coordinates.
- **Scientific Honesty Rule:** Rather than fabricating synthetic millisievert readings, MarsWalk explicitly labels radiation status as:
  > **`UNKNOWN (In-situ surface sensor telemetry unavailable in this sector)`**
