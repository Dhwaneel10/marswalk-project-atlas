## 1. Epistemic Taxonomy Definition

To prevent simulation artifacts from being mistaken for physical facts, every scientific claim across Project Atlas is classified into one of eight strict epistemic tiers:

1. **`DIRECT OBSERVATION`:** Raw, uncalibrated observational telemetry archived in planetary repositories (e.g. raw PDS4 records, raw radiance counts).
2. **`CALIBRATED OBSERVATION`:** Calibrated measurements corrected for instrument transfer functions, geometry, and dark current (e.g. USGS CTX DTM elevation rasters, NASA MEDA Level 3 calibrated temperature/pressure).
3. **`DERIVED OBSERVATION`:** Spatial or physical properties computed directly from calibrated observations via standard mathematical kernels (e.g. Horn 3×3 slopes, line-of-sight raymarching).
4. **`PUBLISHED PHYSICAL MODEL`:** Peer-reviewed, published planetary physics formulations (e.g. MCD GCMNetCDF climate fields, Horn 1981 slope kernel, BEMT rotor aerodynamics, Sabatier reaction thermodynamics).
5. **`CONFIGURED MODEL ASSUMPTION`:** Specific engineering design choices, parameters, and boundary conditions selected for simulation (e.g. 120 kWe Kilopower rating, 250 kWh battery capacity, 4 crew equivalents).
6. **`SYNTHETIC SIMULATION`:** Deterministic outputs generated by running simulation engines forward under configured assumptions (e.g. A* traverse distance, simulated power curves, simulated 60-sol timelines).
7. **`SOFTWARE VERIFICATION RESULT`:** Formal verification of software contracts, test suite pass rates, schema validity, or cryptographic hash continuity (e.g. 355/355 pytest passed, Draft 2020-12 valid).
8. **`UNVERIFIED CLAIM`:** Heuristic statements or extrapolations lacking formal mathematical or empirical validation.

## 3. Mandatory Scientific Guardrails Enforced

1. **No Synthetic Data Masquerading as Real Observations:**
   All synthetic telemetry generated by `step_sol_resources` or `step_mission` is explicitly tagged with `is_simulated = True` or logged in simulation ledgers.
2. **Prohibition of Human Flight Certification:**
   All route plans, life support consumable projections, and structural balances are remote-sensing heuristics. The disclaimer *"Does not certify physical operational safety for human astronauts"* is programmatically enforced across all UI panels and JSON dossiers.
