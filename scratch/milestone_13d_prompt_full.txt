## 3.2 Line-of-sight calculation

For terrain samples along the horizontal path between two endpoints:

1. Compute the horizontal ground path distance.
2. Sample the actual elevation raster along the path.
3. Construct the straight geometric ray between endpoint antenna reference heights.
4. Evaluate each intermediate terrain sample against that ray.
5. Determine whether the terrain obstructs the ray.
6. Record the first obstruction and its location when known.
7. Calculate any geometric clearance information supported by the sampling resolution.

For a normalized horizontal path parameter t:

`P(t) = P0 + t(P1 - P0)`

For the geometric ray height:

`h_LOS(t) = h0 + t(h1 - h0)`

At a terrain sample with elevation `z(t)`, geometric clearance is:

`c(t) = h_LOS(t) - z(t)`

A negative clearance indicates that the sampled terrain surface intersects or exceeds the idealized ray height under this simplified geometric model.

Clearly document the sampling assumptions.

Do not interpret a positive terrain clearance as sufficient Fresnel-zone clearance or proof of RF performance.

Do not hide nodata regions by substituting zero elevation.

If terrain coverage is insufficient to establish visibility, return an explicit `UNKNOWN` or incomplete-data result, not `VISIBLE`.

- Data completeness.
