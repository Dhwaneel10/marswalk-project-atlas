### 1. Scientific & Engineering Limitations

In adherence to NASA scientific integrity guidelines and project change-control rules, this document explicitly identifies the modeling assumptions, resolution constraints, and computational simplifications of MarsWalk 2.0:

#### 1. Topographic Resolution & Sub-Pixel Hazards
- **Current Capability:** Utilizes 1.0 m/pixel USGS HiRISE stereogrammetric DEMs, representing the highest-resolution continuous topographic model currently available from orbit for Mars.
- **Physical Limitation:** Sub-meter hazards—such as sharp centimeter-scale ventifact rocks, 30 cm deep crevices, or unconsolidated regolith sand pockets smaller than 1 meter—cannot be resolved in orbital DEMs.
- **Operational Reality:** An orbital DEM path may appear smooth, but local surface obstacles could require tactical micro-detours. Actual surface missions require forward-looking stereo cameras on the astronaut's helmet or escort rover to perform sub-meter real-time hazard avoidance.

#### 2. Subsurface Geotechnical & Soil Mechanics
- **Current Model:** Terrain traversability is calculated from geometric slope angles and surface roughness heuristics.
- **Physical Limitation:** Does not model granular soil compaction, cohesive shear strength, or wheel sinkage mechanics (e.g., Bekker-Wong terramechanics) across diverse regolith lithologies. A $12^\circ$ slope in basaltic bedrock behaves very differently from a $12^\circ$ active slip face on a basaltic sand ripple (such as in Séítah).

#### 3. Steady-State Physiological & Metabolic Assumptions
- **Current Model:** Applies standardized locomotion equations (adapted from Minetti, Santee, and Pandolf) with constant metabolic efficiency and fixed oxygen consumption rates for walking ($1.0\text{ L/min}$) and stationary sampling ($0.4\text{ L/min}$).
- **Physical Limitation:** Human metabolic rate varies with astronaut fitness, cardiovascular fatigue, thermal comfort, suit joint resistance (pressurized spacesuits significantly increase metabolic cost due to elbow/knee joint torque resistance), and acute psychological stress.

#### 4. Atmospheric & Solar Irradiance Assumptions
- **Current Model:** Weather telemetry uses actual Sol-by-Sol MEDA records and seasonal MCD climatology.
- **Physical Limitation:** Localized microclimate phenomena—such as thermal updrafts at crater rim scarps, turbulent wind gusts, and transient dust devils—are modeled as regional averages rather than high-frequency turbulent simulations.

#### 5. Prominent Non-Safety Operational Notice
> **CRITICAL DISCLAIMER:** MarsWalk 2.0 is an engineering prototype and research decision-support model. It has NOT been certified by NASA Flight Operations Directorate (FOD), the Extravehicular Activity and Human Surface Mobility Program (EHP), or any operational mission authority. Under no circumstances should any route generated by this software be used for real-world spaceflight operations or terrestrial life-critical survival applications without independent operational certification.
